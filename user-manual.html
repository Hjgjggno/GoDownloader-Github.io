📲 How to install an app or game

  1. Open the GoDownloader application on your phone.
  2. Go to the Apps or Games section.
  3. Tap the app or game you want to view.
  4. Read its details and look for the official installation option.
  5. Use only an installation option from a trusted, verified developer. Follow the instructions shown on screen.
  6. To check download progress, swipe down from the top of your phone. This area is called the Notification Panel. A download notification may appear there.
  7. After the download finishes, verify the file source and publisher. Do not open unknown files.
⚠️ Important safety note: Renaming a .bin file to .apk is not a safe or valid installation method. Not every BIN file is an APK. Never rename an unknown file to APK or ignore your phone's security warnings. Install apps only from trusted, official sources.

📚 How to read a book

  1. Open GoDownloader and go to the Books section.
  2. Tap the book you want to read.
  3. If the book is paid, check its price and details first. If you want to buy it, use the official purchase option provided in the app.
  4. If the book is free, tap its Read or Open button.
  5. The book will open in a supported reader. You can now read it.
An internet connection may be required to open a book. Check the price and terms before purchasing.

▶️ How to watch a video

  1. Open GoDownloader and go to the Videos section.
  2. Choose the video you want to watch.
  3. Tap the video card or the Play button.
  4. The video will start in the player. Use the player controls to pause or enter full-screen mode.

🔐 Safety & Help

  • Install apps only from trusted and official sources.
  • Do not open files or pop-ups from unknown websites.
  • Check the app name and developer before installing.
  • Read the price and terms before buying a book.
  • If a button does not work, check your internet connection and reopen the app.

This is a general user guide. Available options may differ depending on your app version and content.
